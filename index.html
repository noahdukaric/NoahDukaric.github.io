About Me

I’m an engineering student driven by a simple curiosity: understanding how things work, measuring what is happening, and finding ways to make them better. My work spans precision surveying and geodetic measurement, CAD and mechanical design, computational analysis, and vehicle performance. Whether I’m building a high-precision control network in the field or developing and testing an engineering model, I enjoy working where theory has to hold up against real-world results.

My interest in vehicle engineering extends beyond the classroom. Through motorsport, sim racing, and track driving, I’ve developed a practical appreciation for vehicle dynamics and performance. Analysing telemetry, braking behaviour, weight transfer, and tire response to understand why a car behaves the way it does. Combined with my background in geomatics and aerospace engineering, this has shaped the way I approach engineering: measure carefully, question the result, test the idea, and keep iterating until the system performs better.
